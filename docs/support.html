Poker Range Trainer - Support

Poker Range Trainer is a preflop range study tool for iOS: build starting-hand ranges on the 13x13 grid and drill them until they stick.

Get help

Email pokerrangetrainer.support@gmail.com and I will reply. Include your app version (shown on the App Store page) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Your data

Everything you create is stored only on your device; there is no account and no cloud sync. To move your library to a new phone, use Account → Export backup on the old device and Import backup on the new one.
